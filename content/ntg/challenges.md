## The "Double Whammy"
[cite_start]Aging and Autism interact bidirectionally: 
* [cite_start]**Aging processes:** Cognitive slowing and physical decline can exacerbate autistic traits like sensory sensitivity and executive dysfunction[cite: 77, 79].
* [cite_start]**Dementia Interaction:** Early signs of dementia may first appear as a sudden loss of "coping skills" or an increase in sensory meltdowns[cite: 12, 17].

---

## Diagnostic Overshadowing
* [cite_start]**The Barrier:** Clinicians may ignore new health issues (like pain or cognitive decline) because they focus only on the developmental disability[cite: 15, 71].
* [cite_start]**Communication:** Non-speaking or minimally-verbal adults may express pain or dementia-related confusion through "challenging behaviors"[cite: 71, 78].
* [cite_start]**Organizational Task:** Staff must be trained to look for *changes* in baseline behavior as medical indicators[cite: 15, 87].
